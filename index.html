```
01 · RESEARCH MOTIVATION

Why study AI integration?

Generative AI is increasingly becoming part of students' academic lives. At XJTLU, students may use AI to search for information, plan coursework, compare alternatives, and make academic decisions.


Our project investigates not only whether AI improves performance, but also whether students feel informed, confident, and in control when AI participates in decision-making.

```
```
02 · FORMATIVE STUDY

Do we need a formative study?

Yes. A formative study can help us understand how XJTLU students currently use AI and which academic tasks represent realistic situations.

01

Participants

8–10 XJTLU undergraduate and postgraduate students who regularly use digital learning tools.

02

Methods

Semi-structured interviews and discussion of common AI-supported academic activities.

03

Purpose

Identify realistic tasks and refine the main experimental procedure.

```
```
03 · RESEARCH DESIGN

3 × 2 Mixed Factorial Design

We investigate two independent variables: AI assistance and AI transparency.

INDEPENDENT VARIABLE 1

AI Assistance

Between-subjects · 3 levels

  • No AI assistance
  • AI suggestion
  • AI generation
INDEPENDENT VARIABLE 2

AI Transparency

Within-subjects · 2 levels

  • Low transparency
  • High transparency

Experimental Conditions

AI Assistance Low Transparency High Transparency
No AI C1 C2
AI Suggestion C3 C4
AI Generation C5 C6
```
```
04 · DEPENDENT VARIABLES

What do we measure?

DV 01

Task Performance

Accuracy, decision quality, and completion time.

DV 02

Perceived Control

Whether students feel they remain in control of the final decision.

DV 03

Trust in AI

Confidence in AI recommendations and explanations.

DV 04

Satisfaction

Satisfaction with the AI-supported decision-making process.

DV 05

AI Reliance

How often participants accept AI recommendations without modification.

QUALITATIVE

Participant Feedback

Interview responses about trust, confusion, rejection, and perceived control.

```
```
05 · BASELINE

A meaningful baseline

The baseline condition removes AI assistance while keeping the task, interface, information resources, and time constraints as comparable as possible.

Student → Information / Interface → Decision

This provides a reference point for understanding changes associated with AI integration.
```
